What this means for you

Coordinating between two homes takes more evenings than anyone plans for: who has the kids when, what is owed, what was agreed.

Should keeps the calendar and the records, drafts the messages, and leaves the sending to you. The details stay where you can find them when they matter.

What it does

  • Schedules and handoffs

    One calendar for custody weekends, school dates and pickups.

  • Shared costs and records

    Kept in order, so the numbers are there when you need them.

  • Drafted messages

    Ready to read and edit. By design, you press send, always.

  • Nothing goes out without you

    By design, messages to other people wait for your yes.
