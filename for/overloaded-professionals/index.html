What this means for you

You hold a job and a household, and the small tasks add up faster than you can clear them. The to-do list never shrinks.

Should keeps your records, tracks what is due, and does the work an agent can do. What reaches you is a short list: the call only you can make, the payment only you can send.

What it does

  • Bills, appointments and due dates

    Kept in one place and tracked for you.

  • Paperwork and follow-ups

    Drafted and followed up for you to send, so you handle the decision, not the legwork.

  • A short list

    Only what needs a person reaches you: a call, a payment, a signature, a choice.

  • You decide

    By design, anything that spends, sends or publishes waits for your yes.
