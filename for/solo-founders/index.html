What this means for you

When you run a company alone, you do every role by hand: the books, the filings, the research, the drafts, the follow-ups.

Should gives you an agent for each of those jobs. They prepare the work, and you stay the one who decides and signs.

What it does

  • Books and numbers

    Prepared by an agent, ready for you to review.

  • Filings and deadlines

    Tracked and drafted ahead of time.

  • Research and drafts

    Written for you to read, edit and approve.

  • You decide and sign

    By design, nothing is filed, paid or sent without your yes.
