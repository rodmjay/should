The ground rules

They work for you, not the other way around

Nothing happens without your yesNothing gets bought, sent, booked or deleted until you approve that exact thing. They fill the cart; you check out.
One question at a timeEvery question comes with three options. No walls of text.
Quiet hoursNo texts between 9 PM and 8 AM, and nothing on Sundays.
No secrets storedNo passwords, no full account numbers. They can't sign in for you.
Everything is loggedEvery change has a name on it, so you can see who did what.
